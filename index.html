🔬 CRITICAL THINKING AND MEDIA LITERACY 🔮🔍

Critical thinking is the ability to analyze information before accepting it as true.

Today, we receive information through social media, websites, videos, and messaging applications. However, not everything online is correct.

Before sharing something, I should ask: Who created this information? Where does it come from? Is it reliable? Can I verify it?

For me, critical thinking helps me make better decisions and become a more responsible digital citizen.

👮 CRIME AND PUNISHMENT 🔎⚖️

Crime is an action that violates the law. Different types of crimes can affect individuals, communities, or society.

Examples include theft, fraud, violence, vandalism, and cybercrime. Punishment depends on the laws of each country and the characteristics of the crime.

Technology has also created new forms of crime, such as online fraud, identity theft, hacking, and misuse of personal information.

Laws and responsible behavior are important for protecting people and maintaining a safe society.
